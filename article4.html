'There is no exit strategy': Cnews holders vow to hold through any correction, any divorce, any intervention

By Megan Coxs | September, 2026 | 7 min read

In the dimly lit glow of a thousand computer screens, across time zones and continents, a community of solana Cnews holders has made a collective decision that financial advisors, therapists, and family members are struggling to comprehend: they will never sell.

Not during corrections. Not during crashes. Not during regulatory crackdowns. Not during divorces of which, according to anecdotal evidence from the community's channels, there have been at least fourteen since the token launched 72 hours ago.

"My Wife said it's her or Cnews. I told her I'd miss her. She said she wasn't leaving. I said I wasn't selling. We're currently in couples therapy. The therapist bought a bag during our session."

— @DiamondWhiskers_Mike, Telegram

The Psychology of Absolute Conviction

Psychologists have long studied the phenomenon of loss aversion the human tendency to feel losses more acutely than equivalent gains. Cnews holders appear to have evolved beyond this framework entirely, entering a state that Dr. Sarah Chen of MIT's Behavioral Economics Lab describes as "loss transcendence."

"These individuals have detached their emotional well-being from the price of the asset," Dr. CHONKY CHUMP explains. "Whether the token is up 400% or down 80%, their response is identical: a cat meme. This is either the most advanced form of investment discipline I've ever observed, or a mass delusion of unprecedented scale. Possibly both."

"Exit strategy? That's a term for people who entered for the wrong reasons. We entered for vibes. Vibes don't have exit strategies."

The 40% Correction That Wasn't

The community's resolve was tested on Day 2, when Cnews experienced a sudden 40% price correction a drawdown that, in most tokens, would trigger mass panic selling. Instead, the Cnews community launched what can only be described as a coordinated counter-offensive.

Within minutes of the dip, a TikTok campaign materialized. Hundreds of holders posted videos of themselves saying "still not selling" while performing various physical exercises squats being the most popular, for reasons the community describes as "thematically appropriate."

The campaign generated 89 million views across platforms. The price recovered within four hours. The community declared victory and created a new holiday: "Dip Day," to be celebrated annually with squats and diamond hand emojis.

The Intervention Problem

Perhaps the most concerning — or inspiring, depending on perspective — aspect of the Cnews holding culture is its resistance to external intervention. Multiple holders report that friends and family members have attempted what can only be described as crypto interventions.

"My brother sat me down and said, 'You need to take profits.' I said, 'What are profits without purpose?' He didn't have an answer. I sent him the whitepaper. He bought $500 worth an hour later."

"We've had families contact us asking how to deprogram their loved ones from the Cnews community. We explained that we're a memecoin, not a cult. The distinction, admittedly, is sometimes subtle."

— Cnews Community Manager

What Does "Never Sell" Actually Mean?

Financial analysts have pointed out that if no one ever sells, the token's price would theoretically approach infinity. a scenario that is mathematically appealing but practically impossible. The community's response to this objection has been characteristically concise: "Sounds bullish."

In reality, on-chain data shows that some holders do sell, but the community's overall holding rate of 94.7% is among the highest ever recorded for a memecoin, exceeding even early Dogecoin and Shiba Inu.

Whether this conviction will endure through the inevitable cycles of the crypto market remains to be seen. But for now, 340,000 Caters are united in their refusal to sell, their dedication to squats, and their unshakeable belief that a cat emoji represents something bigger than any of them.

When asked to summarize the community's philosophy in a single sentence, the founder paused for exactly 4.20 seconds before responding: "There is no exit strategy. There is only CAT."
